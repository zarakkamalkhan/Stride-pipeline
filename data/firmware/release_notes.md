# StrideBand firmware release notes

## 2.4.0 — rolled out from 2026-08-01 (staged; devices update on next charge)
- Optical HR engine now samples at 2 Hz for improved zone accuracy. Cadence and GPS unchanged.
- Auto-pause now triggers after 20 s of no movement (was 45 s).
- Fixed: watch face occasionally showed stale date after a timezone change.
- Companion app 5.2.0 required for sync.

## 2.3.1 — 2026-05-14
- Battery optimisation during long sessions.
- Improved Bluetooth reconnect after the phone goes out of range.

## Companion app 5.2.0 — 2026-08-05
- New sync payload format with richer GPS metadata. Older payloads remain readable on the vendor portal.
